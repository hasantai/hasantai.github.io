CyberVerse

2023 Congressional App Challenge winner, Michigan’s 12th District

My high school had no cybersecurity classes, so I designed the app that would have taught one. CyberVerse is a mobile app built in FlutterFlow that teaches cybersecurity through a ten-unit course with XP points, a friends leaderboard, and a pre-quiz at the start of each unit. A news feed keeps users current on real attacks and scam tactics, and Cybot, a built-in AI chatbot, answers cybersecurity questions and nothing else.

Congresswoman Rashida Tlaib named it the district winner in January 2024. Read the announcement and watch the demo video there.
